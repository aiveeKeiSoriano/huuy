Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Overview

Huuy is a one-time reminder alarm app. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

Huuy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All information you enter — reminder titles and scheduled times — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. No data ever leaves your device.

No Accounts

Huuy does not require an account. There is no registration, login, or user profile of any kind.

No Analytics or Advertising

Huuy does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking tools.

Permissions

Huuy requests the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications

    To alert you when a reminder is due.

  • Alarms & Reminders

    To schedule exact-time alarms via Android's AlarmManager.

  • Lock screen

    To show the alarm on your lock screen when it fires.

  • Boot completed

    To reschedule your alarms after the device restarts.

  • Wake lock / Vibrate

    To wake the screen and vibrate when an alarm fires.


These permissions are used solely to deliver reminders. No permission is used to collect or share data.

Data Deletion

All reminder data can be deleted at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data permanently.

Children's Privacy

Huuy does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be reflected on this page with a revised date.
